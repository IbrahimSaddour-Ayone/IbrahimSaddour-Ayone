## 👋 About me

I'm Ibrahim, a full-stack developer at **AYone**, working across mobile apps and web platforms. My work spans **Flutter applications**, **Angular interfaces**, API integration, dashboards, and business workflows.

I enjoy turning real needs into products people can use every day—from keeping track of warranties to discovering recipes or planning an outdoor adventure. I care about clear navigation, reusable components, and the details that make an application feel good to use.

## 🚀 Selected work

A selection of products I've worked on, spanning everyday mobile utilities and customer-facing web experiences.

### 🧾 Dhakarni

**Warranties, invoices, and reminders in one place.**

A mobile app that helps people organize their products, keep proof of purchase close at hand, and receive reminders before warranties expire or maintenance is due.

[Explore Dhakarni ↗](https://dhakarni.com/) · [View on Google Play ↗](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.gawarranty.dhakarni)

### 🍝 Recettes Italiennes

**Italian cooking, made easy to explore.**

A recipe discovery app built around Italian cuisine, with a modern mobile experience that helps users find inspiration for their next meal.

[Explore Recettes Italiennes ↗](https://www.recettes-italiennes.org/) · [View on Google Play ↗](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.ayone.recettesitaliennes)

### 🏕️ The Adventure Club

**A digital home for outdoor experiences in Tunisia.**

A web platform for discovering outdoor activities, exploring trips and VIP packages, and connecting with a community of adventurers. The experience also brings together corporate team building and a rewards program.

[Explore the platform ↗](https://theadventure-club.com/)
